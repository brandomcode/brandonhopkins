Brandon Hopkins (b. 1997) is a British composer based in London. He writes contemporary classical music, drawing on the minimalism of Steve Reich, Philip Glass, John Tavener, and Arvo Pärt. His work has been featured in films, video games, and installations.



You can listen to his music on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, and more. You can follow him on Instagram, and YouTube. You can support his work on Bandcamp. For licensing, visit Music Vine, or Uppbeat. For all other enquiries, send him an email.
